About the NGCN Research Group

NGCN, led by Dr. Srinivasa Desikan, focuses on research topics in IoT, Machine Learning, Deep Learning, and Edge Computing. The group explores innovative solutions and advancements in these areas to address modern computing challenges. We focus on both theoretical foundations and practical applications, fostering a collaborative environment that encourages creativity and excellence.
